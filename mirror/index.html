These are measurements, not a verdict. "Ideal" means the classical canon or demographic average — a reference line, not a target. The score is a percentile against the SCUT-FBP5500 reference faces (see its card for who they are). Picking several photos at once averages them, which steadies every number. Facial thirds run hairline → brows → nose base → chin; the hairline comes from a hair/skin segmenter because the landmark mesh stops mid-forehead. Lighting, angle and expression all move the numbers.
